# Privacy Policy TapGo

Last updated: 11 Juni 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PT. TapGo Lion Indonesia collects, uses, stores, protects, and processes user data in the TapGo application and related services.

Website: <https://tapgolion.id>  
Support email: support@tapgolion.id

## 1. Data We Collect

TapGo may collect and process the following data:

- Account data: name, phone number, password hash, account status, and role.
- Referral data: referral code, sponsor/upline relationship, downline relationship, and referral activity.
- Membership data: selected package, membership status, membership order, invoice, package benefit, and transaction status.
- Wallet data: application wallet balance, PPOB benefit balance, wallet transaction history, withdrawal request, and bank account data submitted by the user.
- Transaction data: membership payment status, invoice number, payment reference, payment provider response, and payment callback status.
- Contact/support data: messages, support requests, delete account requests, and related correspondence.
- Technical data: device information, app diagnostics, error logs, and security logs needed to protect the service.

TapGo only requests additional data when it is needed for service operation, account verification, support, fraud prevention, or compliance.

## 2. How We Use Data

TapGo uses user data to:

- Create, authenticate, and secure user accounts.
- Manage membership registration, membership status, and package benefits.
- Operate referral reward, commission according to terms, wallet application, PPOB benefit, invoice, and withdrawal flows.
- Process membership payments through approved payment providers.
- Provide customer support and respond to user requests.
- Prevent fraud, misuse, duplicate account abuse, and unauthorized access.
- Maintain audit records required for operational, legal, tax, accounting, or dispute handling purposes.
- Improve app reliability, security, and user experience.

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

User data is stored in systems operated or controlled by TapGo and its authorized service providers. Data is retained as long as needed for:

- Account operation.
- Membership, transaction, wallet, invoice, and withdrawal records.
- Security monitoring and fraud prevention.
- Legal, tax, audit, and accounting obligations.
- Dispute resolution and customer support.

Some financial, transaction, and audit records may need to be retained even after an account deletion request, where required by law or legitimate business recordkeeping.

## 4. Data Security

TapGo applies reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including:

- Password hashing.
- Token-based authentication.
- Role-based access control.
- Secure API communication.
- Operational access controls.
- Logging and monitoring for security and fraud prevention.

No electronic system is completely risk-free, but TapGo works to protect user data from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

## 5. Third-Party Services

TapGo may use third-party services only as needed to operate the application, including:

- Midtrans for membership payment processing and payment notification.
- Google or Firebase services, if enabled, for app diagnostics, notification delivery, analytics, crash reporting, or platform services.
- Hosting, database, storage, and infrastructure providers used to operate TapGo.

Third-party providers receive only the data needed to perform their services. TapGo does not sell personal data.

## 6. User Rights and Account Deletion

Users may request access, correction, update, or deletion of eligible personal data by contacting TapGo support.

Account deletion requests can be sent to:

- Email: support@tapgolion.id
- Website: <https://tapgolion.id>
- In-app delete account menu, if available.

TapGo may verify the request before processing it. Certain transaction, wallet, invoice, withdrawal, audit, tax, and legal records may be retained when required by law or legitimate operational obligations.

## 7. Children's Privacy

TapGo is intended for users who are legally able to register and use membership services. Users must provide accurate information and comply with applicable laws.

## 8. Policy Changes

TapGo may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on the official TapGo website or through other official channels.
