## Slide 12 - Unit Economics

**Illustrative Financial Scenario**

- Paid conversion from registered users starts conservatively at 4%-6%.
- Paid package mix: 80% Silver, 15% Gold, 5% Platinum.

## Slide 14 - Break-even Estimate

**Illustrative Financial Scenario**

Break-even estimate:

- Monthly fixed operating cost assumption: Rp250.000.000-Rp400.000.000.
- Contribution margin assumption after direct benefit/payment cost: 35%-45%.
- Estimated monthly revenue needed for operating break-even: about Rp715.000.000-Rp1.150.000.000.
- Indicative break-even user base depends on paid conversion, package mix, retention, CAC, and recurring platform activity.

Operational levers:

- Improve paid conversion through trusted onboarding.
- Reduce CAC through partner/community acquisition.
- Increase recurring engagement through PPOB and merchant benefits.
- Keep support and operations scalable.

Important note:

This break-even view is a planning model only, not a guaranteed timeline or financial outcome.
