# Investor Deck Design Guide - TapGo

## Brand Direction

TapGo should feel:

- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Modern fintech
- Community business platform
- Operationally credible

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy base | Deep TapGo Navy | `#06142E` |
| Royal blue | TapGo Blue | `#0B4FB3` |
| Electric blue | Action Blue | `#0E64D2` |
| Gold accent | TapGo Gold | `#F5C451` |
| Teal/green | Digital Benefit Green | `#12A67A` |
| Light background | Soft White | `#F7FAFF` |
| Body text | Slate/Navy | `#172033` |

## Typography

Recommended:

- Headline: Inter, SF Pro Display, or Aptos Display.
- Body: Inter, Aptos, or Arial.
- Use strong hierarchy: bold titles, concise subtitles, minimal paragraph density.

## Layout

- 16:9 widescreen.
- Dark navy cover and chapter slides.
- White/soft blue content cards for readability.
- Gold accent for highlights and milestone markers.
- Use charts/tables sparingly and make assumptions explicit.

## Icon Style

- Rounded, solid, modern fintech icons.
- Gold/blue/teal accent system.
- Avoid third-party brand logos unless legally cleared.
- Avoid aggressive money imagery.

## Visual Assets

Use:

- Official TapGo logo: `apps/user_app/assets/images/tapgo_logo.jpeg`
- Feature graphic: `google-play-assets/feature-graphic-1024x500.png`
- Google Play screenshots: `google-play-assets/screenshots/final/`

Avoid:

- Generic Flutter icons as primary brand elements.
- Screenshots showing passwords, tokens, server keys, personal bank accounts, or Midtrans blocker.
- Claims that imply guaranteed earnings.

## Tone

Use investor-safe language:

- membership benefit
- referral reward
- wallet application
- PPOB benefit
- community commerce
- conditional reward
- operational reporting

Avoid high-risk language that implies fixed financial outcomes, prohibited scheme-like claims, or guaranteed results.
