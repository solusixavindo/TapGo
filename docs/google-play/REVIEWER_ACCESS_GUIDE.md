# Reviewer Access Guide - Google Play

Tanggal: 15 Juli 2026

## Reviewer Account

Email/phone identifier: `playreview@tapgolion.id`

Password: do not store in repository.  
Owner should provide the password only through Google Play Console App Access or a secure internal vault.

## Review Scope

Suggested path for reviewer:

1. Open TapGo.
2. Login using reviewer credentials.
3. Open Dashboard.
4. Open Membership packages.
5. Open Checkout/Invoice screen.
6. Open Wallet/TapGoPay.
7. Open Referral Network.
8. Open Account -> Privacy Policy.
9. Open Account -> Terms & Conditions.
10. Open Account -> Hapus Akun.
